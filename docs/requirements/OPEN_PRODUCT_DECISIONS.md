# Product Decision Register

**Status:** Phase 1 decision record. `Resolved` records a locked MVP choice; `Partial` means the stated choice is locked but a named MVP policy remains open; `Open` requires a product decision; `Deferred` is explicitly post-MVP. Original questions and rationale are retained below. No implementation is authorized by this document alone.

| OPD-01 | Who may publish, start, complete, and cancel an event, and can a terminal event reopen? Controls UX, RBAC, audit, and invalid transitions. | Organizer creates/owns the event and alone controls lifecycle, including cancellation from Live. A Cancelled event blocks registration/check-in and retains existing registrations unchanged for audit; participant event-cancellation notification is POST-MVP. | OPD-02 | Which published events can attendees discover, and what details are public? They never expose participant lists/contact, QR credentials, internal operations, live occupancy, alerts, admin data, or audit logs. PRIVATE events are omitted from public discovery. An opaque server-verifiable event-scoped controlled link is both discovery route and bearer authority for the same allowlisted detail only while PRIVATE and Published; possession suffices without login or guest OTP for viewing. At most one link is active per event. Organizer alone issues, revokes, or reissues; replacement immediately invalidates the old proof. No automatic time-based expiry applies in MVP: explicit revoke/replace or leaving PRIVATE Published ends access. A guessed event ID is not access; invalid/unauthorized links and non-Published events receive a privacy-safe unavailable result. | OPD-17 | Which event configuration, staff, correction, alert, certificate, and audit actions belong to Organizer versus Event Admin? Prevents excess access. | Organizer creates/owns event, controls lifecycle including Live cancellation, registration times/manual closure, and Event Admin assignments. ## Additional locked certificate delivery decision

MVP includes explicit single or bulk Organizer/Event Admin issuance from ELIGIBLE registrations, built-in templates and font styles, preview, PDF generation as part of issue, unique certificate IDs, batch/job progress for bulk issue, and auditable revoke. Eligibility alone never generates or sends. A platform sender emails PDF attachments, with Organizer Reply-To permitted. Certificate state and email delivery state are separate: an ISSUED certificate can have a FAILED delivery. Email delivery is PENDING → SENT or FAILED → RETRY; only Organizer/Event Admin may retry a FAILED send, with idempotency for the same certificate/delivery operation. The batch tracks event, template, eligible/generated counts, delivery counts, timestamps, and overall status. No queue, worker, PDF generator, provider, or schema is implemented in Phase 1.

POST-MVP: CSV import, custom template upload/designer/name placement, Organizer Gmail OAuth sending, duration-based eligibility, and other external notification channels. Emailing an issued certificate is part of the MVP certificate workflow, not a general alert/notification channel.

## Remaining approval and technical decisions

No MVP-blocking product choice remains in this register. Terminal event reopening, registration amendment, direct staff credential control, and unrestricted exports are outside the locked MVP flows and require a later explicit decision if proposed. Authentication/session mechanism, OTP provider and abuse limits, token format, scan-key retention, WebSocket/SSE, queue/provider choice, Kafka, deployment, workload, data retention, and forecasting cadence/minimum-data/production threshold remain technical or later-phase decisions. The original option history in the earlier Phase 1 draft was: lifecycle Organizer/Admin/both and reopening; discovery catalog/link/invitation; identity account/verified guest; cancellation cutoff/admin-only and waitlist/no waitlist; duplicate verified account/normalized identity; check-out required/optional; re-entry none/after exit/bounded; correction reasoned/no MVP correction; volunteer read-only/task status; certificate accepted attendance/duration/other rule; alerts capacity/gate outage/staleness; registration automatic/manual and Live/no Live; gate decision-only/limited cue; gate pause/escalate/manual; capacity warn/registration cap/gate cap/combination. Locked choices above supersede that option list where they speak to it.
